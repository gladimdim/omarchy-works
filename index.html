SUPER DESKTOP

Every AI agent. One shortcut away.

  • Press SUPER SHIFT Q and your coding agents slide in as live terminal cards with sticky notes. Press it again and they hide but keep running.
  • Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Grok and more, each in its own card. A Files button previews images, PDFs and Markdown the agent mentions.
  • Follows your Omarchy theme. Open another PC's agents from yours, or all of them from your phone, over your own network. No accounts.
  • A command line and an MCP server let agents drive it too.
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gladimdim/super-desktop/master/install.sh | bash

Omarchy plugin · SteamOS · Android app · Web editor · MIT

Omakey

Your phone is the keyboard. A real one.

  • Turns an Android phone into a physical keyboard and touchpad for Omarchy and the Steam Deck: a kernel-level device, so Hyprland shortcuts, games and even the lock screen work, in Game Mode too.
  • Layouts of real keyboards: laptop QWERTY, Colemak and Dvorak, and split boards like the Corne, Lily58, ErgoDox and Kinesis Advantage, each half under a thumb. Or design your own in Layout Studio.
  • About one encrypted packet per key over Wi-Fi, paired with a QR code. Falls back to Bluetooth, and works as a plain Bluetooth keyboard for any computer. Portrait mode types with your own Android keyboard.
  • Listed and verified on the Omarchy plugin marketplace. The bar widget pairs phones and shows a QR code for the app.

Garmin Connect IQ watch face · coming soon

Omarchy for Garmin

A clean face for 60 Garmin watches.

  • The weekday and date, the square Omarchy mark, the OMARCHY wordmark and a clock in the same block lettering. Nothing else.
  • From the one-bit Instinct to round AMOLED fenix, epix, Venu and Forerunner, and the rectangular Venu X1. On Instinct, the mark sits in the round sub-window.
  • An always-on mode for AMOLED screens that drifts so it never burns in. No permissions, no network, no sensors.

On its way to the Connect IQ store.

Omarchy plugins

Small things for the bar.

Install any of them with one command. They live in your Omarchy bar and follow your theme.

Config Sync

Most starred

Sync your configs, shortcuts, themes and plugins between machines through your own private git repo. See every change before you apply it, with a timestamped backup each time. Publish pushes this machine's changes, and drift shows up so you can pick what to keep.

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/gladimdim/omarchy-config-sync-plugin --enable --yes

AI quotas in your dock

Omarchy AI Usage Bar

Old-school ASCII progress bars in the Omarchy dock for Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Antigravity and other providers' usage, quotas and rate limits. Pick from six bar styles and track up to twelve limits, with a popup dashboard for the rest.

An animated banner tells you when a limit runs out or resets. It makes no network requests of its own.

omarchy plugin install https://github.com/gladimdim/omarchy-ai-usage-bar.git --enable

Hardware Info

CPU temperature, fan speed, RAM and disk in the tray, and a six-tab inspector for everything else. Copy a full JSON report with one key.

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/gladimdim/omarchy-hardware-info-plugin --enable

Air Raid Alert · Ukraine

A live air-raid alarm for your oblast: the trident in the bar turns green or blinks red, with a notification and a siren. No account or token needed.

omarchy plugin install https://github.com/gladimdim/omarchy-air-raid-alert-ukraine-plugin.git --enable

Omarchy on the Steam Deck

OmaDeck

Fixes and tweaks that make Omarchy great on a Steam Deck, switched on and off from a gamepad icon in the bar. Plug in a monitor or TV and the Deck screen turns off; unplug it and it comes back. Every fix stays in your own config and can be undone.

Docked? The panel's Companion apps link Omakey, so your phone becomes the Deck's keyboard and touchpad.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gladimdim/OmaDeck/main/install.sh | bash

Today Reminders
